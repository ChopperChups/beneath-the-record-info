This is the information page for the channel owner's documentary production and YouTube publishing application. It is a personal production tool, not a public service that invites viewers to connect their Google accounts.

Privacy · Use of this application · YouTube channel
Updated 19 October 2026

Privacy and Google account access

The channel owner authorizes this application through Google's OAuth consent screen. The application does not request, receive, or store the owner's Google password.

What the application uses

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens to maintain the owner's authorized connection.
  • The connected channel's identity, video metadata, publication status, and aggregate video statistics.
  • Files and metadata produced for the channel: documentaries, Shorts, thumbnails, captions, source records, rights evidence, and scheduled publication dates.

It does not collect viewers' Google credentials or individual viewing histories. The current controller does not automatically send comment replies, delete videos, buy advertising, or change channel ownership or security settings.

Why this information is used

The application uses these records to verify the destination channel, upload and schedule approved production outputs, add thumbnails and captions, prevent duplicate uploads, and assess the channel's own video performance. Automated quality checks can block publication.

Storage and sharing

The owner operates the production service on a private server. Authentication files are stored separately from production workspaces with restrictive filesystem permissions. Transfers to Google and between the owner's computers and server use encrypted connections. Production files and operational records remain on the owner's systems unless uploaded to the owner's YouTube channel.

Google receives the authorized API requests and uploaded content. The production assistant uses the owner's existing OpenAI/Codex account for research and content production. Credentials are not intentionally included in production prompts or public output. Aggregated channel performance may inform production decisions. Google account data is not sold, used for advertising profiles, or used to train general-purpose AI models by this application.

The application’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

The owner controls the server and can stop the service or remove its records. Authorization tokens are retained while the connection is in use. Production files and operational records are retained for the owner's channel management, attribution and troubleshooting until the owner removes them. YouTube retains published content and records under its own policies.

You can revoke the application’s Google access through your Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized API access; it does not itself delete videos already uploaded to YouTube or files already stored by the owner. The owner can separately remove local credentials, retained application data, or YouTube content.

This public information page

This page contains no sign-in form, advertising, analytics scripts, or application cookies. The hosting provider may process standard connection information, such as IP addresses, for delivering and securing the page. Its own privacy terms apply to that processing.

Contact

Questions about this application or the owner's retained records can be directed to the owner through the Beneath the Record channel information. This application is operated for that channel's own use.

Use of this application

This is a private production tool for its channel owner. No paid subscription, viewer account, or public authorization service is offered on this page. The owner is responsible for reviewing content rights, factual accuracy and channel settings. Automated checks do not guarantee publication performance, monetization or uninterrupted operation.

Use of YouTube remains subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Google account authorization can be withdrawn at any time through the link above.
